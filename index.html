Case Study
RailClerk Live

Railway logistics platform that replaced Excel chaos with a single operating system for wagon fleet management across Kazakhstan and CIS. One platform, one source of truth — from movement tracking to supplier billing.

The Problem
01 Wagon movements tracked across dozens of conflicting Excel files
02 Manual day-counting for rental billing — errors led to supplier disputes
03 No audit trail — confirmed costs could be silently modified
04 Zero visibility into per-ride profitability across multiple suppliers
What I Built
Movement Engine
Async Excel ETL pipeline. Upload daily wagon data, auto-parse columns, resolve station codes, link movements to rides.
Batch Creator
Auto-groups wagon journeys from loaded to empty. Real batches from movements + synthetic batches for transit-only scenarios.
Day Counting Engine
450+ lines of billing logic. Rental days respecting loan periods, subtracting repair windows, monthly rate breakdowns.
Cost Aggregation
Unified supplier fees across wagon rental, local transit, international transit, and auxiliary services.
Confirmation Lock
Ride confirmation creates audit snapshots, locks linked costs, logs every blocked mutation. Director-only reversal.
RBAC + Multi-Tenant
Director / Supervisor / Specialist hierarchy. Financial visibility rules filter data at query level per role.
Node / Express PostgreSQL SvelteKit TypeScript
